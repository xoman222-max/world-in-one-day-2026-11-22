1 ANTALYA · CLIMATE REPORTED

COP31 ENDS WITHOUT A PACT

After two weeks plus two days of overtime — the longest UN climate summit on record — Antalya ended Sunday with no climate pact. Final text omits "fossil fuels"; EU, Panama and Marshall Islands object; poorer states want clear adaptation-finance figures. President Murat Kurum: "these decisions have not been adopted". Guterres: "deeply disappointed... no ambiguity in the science."
