COP31: NO PACT REPORTED
1 ANTALYA, TÜRKIYE · CLIMATE

Climate talks end without a pact

After two weeks and two days of overtime, the “Antalya Pact” was not adopted: the EU, UK, Panama, Marshall Islands and others objected to a text that does not mention fossil fuels. Kurum: “these decisions have not been adopted.” Guterres: “deeply disappointed”. Next talks: COP32, Ethiopia.

Sources: UN News · Reuters · Guardian · ENB

2 DYMER, KYIV REGION · WAR

Russian strike kills 9 in apartment block

Ukraine's air force says Russia launched 186 drones overnight and 161 were downed. A five-storey block in Dymer was hit at 05:00; a boy, 7, was pulled alive after 14 hours. Zelensky says 3 died at a Vyshneve grocery.

Sources: Ukrinform · BBC · Guardian

3 MANAMA, BAHRAIN · IRAN WAR

Bahrain rejects Iran's radar claim

Iran's Guards say missiles and drones hit two US Patriot radars at Riffa and Awali in Saturday night's attack. On Sunday Bahrain said a civilian industrial site in Sitra burned, 2 people were hurt, and asked the UN Security Council to act. Neither account is independently verified.
